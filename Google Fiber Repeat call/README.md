📞 Google Fiber – Customer Support Repeat Call Analysis

Uncovering why customers call back — and what we can do about it.

Buruhani Mutebi — Data Analyst
Data Period: January – March 2022
Tools Used: SQL , Power BI, Microsoft PowerPoint

📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes Google Fiber customer support repeat call data across three markets and five issue types over Q1 2022. The goal is to identify patterns in repeat contact behavior, pinpoint the highest-impact problem areas, and provide actionable recommendations to improve First-Call Resolution (FCR).


📈 Trends (Jan – Mar 2022)

All three markets show a gradual upward trend in repeat calls from January to March.
market_1 consistently drives the highest monthly volume (~4,100–4,350 calls/month).
market_2 remains stable and low across all months.


✅ Recommendations

Focus on type_5 & type_2 — Conduct root-cause analysis and build targeted knowledge-base articles and agent scripts for these two issue categories, which together drive 80% of repeat calls.
Prioritize market_1 — With 62.5% of all repeat calls, market_1 needs dedicated FCR improvement initiatives including coaching sessions, escalation reviews, and quality audits.
Study market_2 Best Practices — market_2 low repeat rate (3.9%) suggests effective resolution practices. Identify and replicate those practices across market_1 and market_3.
Track Week-over-Week Trends — Build real-time dashboards segmented by week and quarter to detect spikes early and measure the impact of improvement initiatives.
